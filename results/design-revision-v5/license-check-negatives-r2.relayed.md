3. MOST CONSEQUENTIAL EDIT, ARG-RETURN-04 (Apollonius, Argonautica 4.1694-1698, the 'pall' darkness). The drafter put the darkness on Night 0, and all options (a, b, c, none) were night readings. But the heroes embark at the new dawn (4.1690-1692), and the text continues 'Αὐτίκα δὲ Κρηταῖον ὑπὲρ μέγα λαῖτμα θέοντας νὺξ ἐφόβει' (at once, as they ran over the Cretan sea, night terrified them). No sunset is narrated in between, so the onset is not stated. The darkness is also 'νύκτʼ ὀλοὴν', the phrase of the Iliad's daytime darkness at Il. 16.567, which this same file reads as a solar eclipse in IL-PATROCLUS-02. EDITS: (1) the statement now says the onset is unstated, in the daylight of Day 0 or at its nightfall; (2) 'Αὐτίκα …' is added to the licence (ref 4.1694 was already cited); (3) new option d: 'a solar eclipse of class X1-X4 (DESIGN 3.1) visible at the set's first site (the Cretan sea beyond Cape Salmonis) on Day 0, maximum at any time the Sun is above the horizon', marked as the checker's inference, with the case against it stated (the text calls it night, names stars and Moon as unseen, and makes it last to dawn, 4.1713-1714); (4) set level: ARG-RETURN-04:d is added to eclipse_compatible_options, and day0, the first place label and both slot notes are reworded. The darkness note had wrongly said the darkness was eclipse-compatible 'only as a lunar eclipse', although option b, a conjunction, was already listed. This changes ARG-RETURN's hit_j under DESIGN 6.5, since it is a Day-0 eclipse option evaluated at the first site.

4. AEN-TROY-02 (Aeneid 2.255, 'tacitae per amica silentia lunae'). The menu had readings i (the Moon up), ii (the Moon at conjunction) and none. It lacked a reading the words admit: a 'silent' Moon that gives no light because it is not up when the fleet sails after nightfall ('et iam', 2.254, follows 2.250-253). This is the only reading under which 2.255 agrees with 'per lunam' at 2.340 (row 04); reading ii contradicts 2.340, which is why the file needs both R-ii and R-ii-literal. EDITS: new option iii, 'the Moon is below the horizon at the end of evening nautical twilight of Night 0 (any phase)'. The end-of-twilight instant is the checker's stand-in for 'after nightfall', and the justification says so. Option iii is not eclipse-compatible. A new pinned reading R-iii is added (02:iii, 04:a, 05:none, 07:vis7). The day0_phase note and the set notes are updated. Pliny's 'silentis lunae' = day of conjunction is verified at pliny-nh-lat.tsv 16.39.2, and 18.31.3 and 28.7.3 also check.
