3. **The method is not shown to see**, split by component [rev #3]:
   - **3a, the eclipse component.** The method does not recover the dates of
     real eclipse records (PC-R, section 6.3).
     - It is scored on four legs: the as-licensed primaries and a
       words-only projection, each scored all-must-pass and by best fit.
     - 3a holds if **any** leg counts fewer than 4 of the 7 counted sets
       seen (6.3.2) [r1v5 N1, N7].
   - **3b, the B&M-type component** (lunar phase, morning star and Mercury
     turning point). The method does not recover the dates of real
     planetary and lunar records of B&M's own kinds, at observer slack
     calibrated on the *other* records (the *Almagest* control, section
     6.4).
     - It is scored all-must-pass and by best fit, each at three rounding
       steps of the slack ceilings, under the frozen meaning of
       `same_apparition` (10.3). 3b holds if **any** of these six legs
       counts fewer than 6 of the 11 counted sets seen [r2v6 N1, N6].
     - B&M's fourth kind, the star season, has no dated real record, so that
       component is **untested** [r1v5 N13; rev #3 fix 4].
   - **Whether a gate could have passed** is a null-side question
     [r2v6 N1]. A set *can be narrowed* if its projection holds a window's
     candidates to 5% in most null windows, whatever its truth. If fewer
     counted sets can be narrowed than a gate's threshold, Q_attain3a or
     Q_attain3b holds. The label is then printed as "untested by these
     controls" rather than "not shown to see".

   Either way, every "no" about the Odyssey that depends on the failing
   component is reported as "could not have seen it". Revision 6 named the
   label "the method cannot see". A gate that fails shows only that the
   controls did not demonstrate sight, so the label now says that, and each
   gate prints its per-set breakdown beside it (6.3.2, 6.4).
4. **The method dates fiction.** The eclipse-matching method, applied to a
   clean negative (fiction composed long after the events it tells), yields
   under the negative's own sourced readings an eclipse match at least as
   strong as the Odyssey's (hit_j). Its significance must also be at least
   as great, with each side at the bound least favourable to the claim: the
   negative's upper bound against the Odyssey's lower bound,
   G_j,hi ≤ G_BM,u,lo [r2v6 N8]. That retires the eclipse-matching method,
   not only the claim. Whether any clean negative *could* meet both
   conditions is a null-side question, settled before the target is looked
   at (Q_attain4, 6.5) [r2v6 #71].
