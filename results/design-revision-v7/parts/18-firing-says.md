**What a firing says, set by set** [r2v6 N1]. On the known numbers 3a fires
(2.10). Part of the reason is the records, not the method: two counted sets
cannot be narrowed, and two others carry errors in their own words
(AppT 6). Revision 6 said so in a sentence that no quantity computed.
Revision 7 prints the reason mechanically. Beside each gate the verdict
prints, for every counted set and leg:

- whether the set can be narrowed (null side);
- whether its truth is kept: f(truth) = 0 for a strict leg, the truth in B
  for a best-fit leg, with the rows its truth fails;
- whether it is seen;
- and the number of sets seen among those that can be narrowed.

The design does not repair the gate by dropping weak sets or lowering the
threshold. Any such change would now be chosen with the answers in view, and
it would favour the claim that the method can see. The count among the
narrowable sets is printed beside the gate, and it enters no rule.
