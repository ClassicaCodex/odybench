all 25 issues of the first review resolved in their own terms, 14 of the 15
issues of the recheck of revision 5 resolved, and #71 not resolved in
substance. It raised 10 new issues: one blocker, two major and seven minor
[r2v6 §0]. Revision 7 answers each one, and #71 below the table. Where an
answer here changes a resolution in 14.1–14.5, this table is the current one.
