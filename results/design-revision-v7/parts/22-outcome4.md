- **Outcome 4 fires if some clean negative has hit_j and
  G_j,hi ≤ G_BM,u,lo** [r2v6 N8]. G_BM,u is the Odyssey's G under 𝒢_BM* over
  T, the same kind of unconditioned target set. It is the Odyssey's
  coincidence as B&M would present it, with every reading treated as blind.
  The comparison favours B&M three times: 𝒢_j holds more readings than
  𝒢_BM*, the negative is taken at its upper bound, and the Odyssey at its
  lower bound. Revision 6 compared with the point value G_BM,u, which made
  the comparison up to 1.6 times looser than the claim allows: 0.00172
  against 0.00107 on the design-stage numbers (2.9).
- **Whether outcome 4 could fire at all** is known at the second freeze
  [r1v5 N5; r2v6 #71]. Outcome 4 needs two things of one negative, and
  `attain.py` measures, on the null side, whether any negative could supply
  both.
  - **The G condition: 0 < G_j and G_j,hi ≤ G_BM,u,lo.** A unique survivor
    in the core has reach > 0. So the garden must make some targets unique,
    but few enough: about 11 reach-units over the 10,690 targets of the core.
    The gardens differ in size: 294 readings for AEN-TROY, 90 for
    ARG-RETURN and 32 for QS-SACK, before F5 expansion, against 𝒢_BM*'s 36
    (2.6).
  - **The eclipse condition: E_j ≥ 1.** E_j is the number of conjunctions u
    of the core with h_tot(u) ≥ m̂, at the eclipse-compatible option's own
    site and day, that some eclipse-compatible reading of 𝒢_j makes the
    unique survivor of a 136- or 251-year window containing it (reach > 0,
    on the mapped survivors above). m̂ = 0.304 is the design-stage value of
    M_Ody (2.3), frozen as its null-side stand-in, because M_Ody itself
    reads the target (4.4). E_j at 0.2 and at 0.4 is reported beside it.
  - **Why both.** Revision 6 read the G condition alone. G_j averages over
    the whole garden, mostly readings that are not eclipse-compatible, so a
    negative could meet it while no eclipse-compatible reading reaches any
    eclipse [r2v6 #71]. Revision 5 had read an even weaker condition,
    G_BM,u > U0(n_j).
  - **Q_attain4** holds if no clean negative meets both conditions. "No
    label 4" then means nothing, and the verdict says so.
